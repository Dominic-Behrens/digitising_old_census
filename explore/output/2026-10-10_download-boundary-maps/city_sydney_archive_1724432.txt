City of Sydney Ward Map, 1908-1924
Expand/collapse

Date
Between 22nd December 1908 and 2nd October 1924

Description
Wards include Gipps, Belmore, Bligh, Bourke, Camperdown, Cook, Denison, Fitzroy, Flinders, Macquarie, Phillip, Pyrmont. Denison Ward was split to cover Ultimo, and Pyrmont Ward was created to cover Pyrmont. LGA boundaries were extended from Camperdown to part of Marrickville which became the new Camperdown Ward.
