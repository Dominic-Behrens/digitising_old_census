City of Sydney Ward Map, 1932-1948
Expand/collapse

Unique ID

Date
Between 22nd December 1932 and 31st December 1948
Date qualifier
exact
Format
Map
Description
Wards include Gipps, Fitzroy, Flinders, Macquarie, Phillip. Gipps Ward extended to Haymarket Subsuming Lang Ward. Phillip Ward was extended to cover the former Pyrmont and Camperdown Wards. Fitzroy Ward gained back part of Woolloomooloo and Denison Ward disappeared. Macquarie Ward took over Bourke and Belmore Wards, and extended down to Surry Hills. Cook Ward was taken over by Flinders Ward which also took over Bligh Ward.
