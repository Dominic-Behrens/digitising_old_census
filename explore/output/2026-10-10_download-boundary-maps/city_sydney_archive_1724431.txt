City of Sydney Ward Map, 1902-1908
Expand/collapse

Date
Between 20th August 1902 and 21st December 1908

Description
Wards include Gipps, Belmore, Bligh, Bourke, Cook, Denison, Fitzroy, Flinders, Lang, Macquarie, Phillip. Brisbane Ward was renamed Lang Ward. Belmore and Bligh Wards were added. Cook Ward was divided and Moore Park was covered by the new Flinders Ward.
