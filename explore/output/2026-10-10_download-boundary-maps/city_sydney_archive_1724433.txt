City of Sydney Ward Map, 1924-1932

Description
Wards include Gipps, Belmore, Bligh, Bourke, Camperdown, Cook, Fitzroy, Flinders, Macquarie, Phillip, Pyrmont. Denison Ward was moved to cover Woolloomooloo, splitting Fitzroy Ward. Pyrmont Ward extended to cover the suburb Ultimo.
