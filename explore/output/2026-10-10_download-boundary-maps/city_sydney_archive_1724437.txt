City of Sydney Ward Map, 1959-1968

Description
Wards include Gipps, Fitzroy, Northcott, Phillip. Alexandria, Darlington, Erskineville, Newtown, Redfern, and Waterloo became part of the Northcott Ward. Paddington and Flinders Wards became part of Fitzroy Ward. Leichhardt became part of Phillip Ward. Macquarie became part of Gipps Ward.
